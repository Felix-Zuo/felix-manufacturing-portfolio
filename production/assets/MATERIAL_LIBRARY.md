## Distribution boundary

- Raw manufacturer CAD, Twinmotion/Fab source assets, and downloaded texture
  archives stay local.
- Public delivery contains only screenshots, video renders, and project-authored
  code/data permitted by the source licenses.
- Every new external asset must be added here before it is used in an approved
  scene.
