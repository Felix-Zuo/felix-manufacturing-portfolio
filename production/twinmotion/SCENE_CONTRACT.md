# V5 Twinmotion Scene Contract

## Purpose

Rebuild the approved bright manufacturing reference as a controlled 30-second cinematic. Twinmotion owns final environment dressing, PBR materials, daylight, camera sequencing, and final rendering. Blender owns only dimensional blockout and mechanical animation.

## Spatial Contract

- Factory bay: approximately 36 m long, 18 m wide, 9 m clear height.
- Camera spine: a straight 3.2 m service aisle with a clean central vanishing point.
- Left foreground: fenced six-axis handling cell. The robot sweep envelope never crosses the fence or aisle.
- Left midground: enclosed bearing grinding machine with a credible service door and observation window.
- Right foreground: recessed 16:9 project display integrated into a structural wall bay, never floating.
- Center: one low-profile AGV, moving away from camera at a constant low speed.
- Background: repeated enclosed machine cells and restrained status towers, not decorative clutter.
- Floor markings: one clear yellow route with no raised rail or transverse obstruction.
- Ceiling: believable portal frames, skylights, luminaires, cable trays, and limited organized pipework.

## Process Contract

The grinding insert shows an outer ring held vertically with a horizontal work axis. A small profiled internal grinding wheel enters the bore and contacts the inner raceway. The machine remains enclosed. Use coolant mist and a restrained contact glint, never a welding-style shower of sparks.

## Camera Contract

- Output: 30 seconds, 24 fps, one continuous forward screen-direction.
- Lens family: 28-35 mm full-frame equivalent; no extreme wide-angle distortion.
- Height: 1.55-1.70 m, level horizon except for deliberate millimetric easing.
- Motion: dolly and crane only; no orbit, handheld shake, roll, whip pan, or focus pumping.
- All speed changes use long ease-in/ease-out curves and preserve forward momentum.

| Time | Camera and action |
| --- | --- |
| 0-5 s | Establish the bright factory spine and gently enter the aisle. AGV moves away slowly. |
| 5-10 s | Pass the fenced robot cell. One measured pick-and-place action, then a short bullet-time slowdown. |
| 10-15 s | Reveal the enclosed grinding cell through its window. Slow near the verified raceway contact. |
| 15-20 s | Move to the first recessed project screen. Camera becomes nearly orthogonal for DOM takeover. |
| 20-25 s | Continue to operations visibility and maintain readable screen geometry. |
| 25-30 s | Finish square to the takt simulator screen with a stable hold for interactive handoff. |

## Look Contract

- Neutral daylight around 5200-6000 K with soft skylight directionality.
- Graphite, white powder coat, aluminium, clear safety glass, restrained safety yellow.
- Clean working factory, not sterile science fiction and not an abandoned warehouse.
- Slightly used surfaces only at contact points; no grime layers, dramatic smoke, or orange/teal grade.
- Small, recessive Felix Zuo identity. No large text baked into the render.

## Delivery Contract

- Master: 1920x1080 PNG sequence or visually lossless mezzanine.
- Web desktop: seek-friendly H.264 MP4, fixed short GOP, faststart, target <=18 MB.
- Web mobile: dedicated 1080x1920 composition when possible, target <=12 MB.
- Project UI is never generated or baked into the 3D render. Screens are dark neutral glass and real DOM takes over in the website.
