## Strong Outcome Metrics

These are central to the portfolio.

### Production Notice Workflow

Old process:
Production notice preparation required repeated manual checks across TC, ERP, BOM records, inventory, process requirements, production planning, and delivery information. It often depended on experienced users.

Outcome:
Preparation time reduced from around 20-40 minutes to under 1 minute.

Additional effect:
Less-experienced users can generate standard-compliant production notices. Manual copying, missing information, repeated checking, and coordination errors are reduced.

### Trial Production Takt Simulation And Changeover Improvement

During trial production, Felix worked with engineering teams to simulate full-line takt time, import machine parameters, and identify mass-production bottlenecks before ramp-up.

Outcome:
Trial analysis and adjustment cycle shortened from around 3 days to 1 day.

Scrap outcome:
Trial machining and debugging scrap material reduced by around 90%.

Cost outcome:
For a regular model changeover, estimated saving reached around RMB 20,000 per changeover across inner/outer rings, auxiliary components, consumables, and labor efficiency.

Use the word "estimated" for the RMB saving unless later supported by audited finance records.
